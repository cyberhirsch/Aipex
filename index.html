How the score works. Each source's scores are rescaled within a category so its leader is 100 and its 15th place is 50, halving again for every further gap of the same size. A model's Aipex score is the mean of those values across every source that ranks it; where several sources cover a category, models missing from some of them are pulled slightly toward 50, so a model that leads on every board beats one that leads on a single board. Hover a score to see each source's raw value. The VRAM filter shows open-weight models that fit the chosen memory at a practical quantisation (usually 4-bit for LLMs, FP8 for image and video models) — an estimate, not a guarantee.
